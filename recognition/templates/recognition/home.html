How It Works

Three simple steps to fully automated attendance

1. Register Faces

Admin captures 200 photos per employee via browser webcam. No special hardware required.

2. Train AI Model

SVM model trained on 128-dim face encodings using dlib deep learning network.

3. Mark Attendance

Employees are recognized in real-time. Attendance logged automatically with timestamps.
